# E-Commerce Market Analysis in Colombia

## Project Overview

This project explores the evolution of Colombia's e-commerce market through exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical methods, and data visualization. Using retail sales data from Colombia's National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE), the project investigates online shopping trends, product category performance, and the relationship between e-commerce and traditional retail sales.

The goal is to identify patterns in the Colombian retail market, understand changes in sales over time, and generate data-driven insights into the growth of digital commerce.

## Objectives

- Analyze retail sales trends across different product categories.
- Explore the evolution of e-commerce and traditional retail channels over time.
- Clean, transform, and prepare datasets for statistical analysis.
- Apply data normalization and standardization techniques.
- Identify outliers and examine variable distributions.
- Use statistical tests and regression analysis to investigate patterns and temporal trends.
- Create visualizations to communicate findings and support data-driven conclusions.

## Methodology

1. **Data Collection:** Retrieved retail sales and e-commerce datasets from DANE.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handled missing values, corrected data types, and prepared date variables.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined descriptive statistics, distributions, and potential outliers.
4. **Data Preprocessing:** Applied Min-Max normalization and Z-score standardization.
5. **Statistical Analysis:** Investigated variable distributions, relationships, and trends over time.
6. **Data Visualization:** Created charts to explore sales behavior and communicate analytical findings.
7. **Regression Analysis:** Explored linear regression to examine temporal trends in retail and e-commerce indicators.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python — data analysis and visualization.
- Pandas — data manipulation and preprocessing.
- NumPy — numerical operations.
- Matplotlib and Seaborn — data visualization.
- Jupyter Notebook / Google Colab — interactive analysis environment.
- DANE datasets — source of Colombian retail and e-commerce statistics.

## Expected Outcomes

The project provides an analytical overview of retail sales behavior in Colombia, highlights temporal variations and differences between online and traditional sales channels, and demonstrates a data analysis workflow from raw datasets to statistical exploration and visual communication.

## Data Source

National Administrative Department of Statistics (DANE), Colombia.
